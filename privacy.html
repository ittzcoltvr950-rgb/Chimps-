Effective date: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Chimps! A New Legacy ("the Game", "we", "us") collects when you play on Meta Quest, how we use it, and the choices you have. The Game is published and managed by AustraX. This policy is available at this public web address and is linked from the Game's page on the Meta Horizon Store. By playing the Game, you agree to this policy.

In short

  • We collect only what is needed to run the Game and its online multiplayer: your Meta display name, in-game movement and actions, connection data, and voice chat while you are in a lobby.
  • We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.
  • Voice chat is live only and is not recorded or stored.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing colt3211@gmail.com (see Section 7).

1. Information we collect

Information from your Meta account. When you play on Meta Quest, the platform may give us limited information such as your Meta user ID, display name or username, and avatar. We use this only to run the Game and, if you play online, to identify you to other players. We do not receive your password or payment details.

Gameplay and multiplayer data. When you play online (for example, in Freeplay lobbies or the 4-player Horror mode), we process:

  • Your player name and the room or lobby you join
  • Real-time data needed to show you to other players, such as head and hand positions, movement, and in-game actions
  • Your IP address and basic connection data, which are needed to connect you to multiplayer servers
  • Shared game state, such as story objectives completed by your team

Voice chat and microphone. The Game asks for microphone permission so you can talk to other players in your lobby. Your voice is transmitted in real time to those players and is used for nothing else. We do not record or store voice audio, and you can mute yourself or turn off your microphone at any time in your headset settings.

Settings stored on your device. Your settings (turn style, turn speed, music and sound volume, comfort vignette, and low performance mode) are saved locally on your headset. They are not sent to us.

Diagnostic and performance data. The Game or its services may collect crash reports and basic technical information (device model, operating system version, app version, performance data) to fix bugs and improve stability.

Leaderboards and scores. If the Game offers leaderboards or competitions, we store your display name, scores, and times so they can be shown to other players.

Hand tracking and sensors. The Game uses your headset's controller and head tracking, and hand tracking where you enable it, to function. This data is processed on your device to control gameplay. We do not collect or store body, face, or eye tracking data, or images of your surroundings.

2. How we use information

We use the information above to:

  • Run the Game and connect you with other players
  • Keep multiplayer sessions in sync
  • Prevent cheating, abuse, and harassment
  • Fix bugs, measure performance, and improve the Game
  • Provide leaderboards and competitions (if included)
  • Respond to support requests
  • Comply with legal obligations and platform requirements

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your information for advertising.

3. Third-party services

The Game relies on third-party services, which process data on our behalf under their own privacy policies:

  • Meta Platforms: the Meta Quest platform, account, and store (privacy policy)
  • Photon (Exit Games): multiplayer networking (legal information)
  • PlayFab (Microsoft): backend services such as leaderboards and player data, if the Game uses them (privacy policy)

4. Other players

When you play online, other players in your lobby can see your name and avatar, and see and hear what you do in the game. Please do not share personal information, such as your real name, address, or phone number. Meta also provides tools to block and report players.

5. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. Real-time multiplayer data is generally discarded when you leave a session. Leaderboard entries and account-linked data (if any) are kept until you ask us to delete them or until they are no longer needed.

6. Children's privacy

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Meta sets its own minimum age requirements for Meta Quest accounts and offers parent-managed accounts; those rules also apply when playing the Game. If you believe a child has given us personal information without proper consent, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict certain processing, and withdraw consent where we rely on it. You can also manage data permissions and delete your account data through your Meta account settings.

How to request deletion of your data. To ask us to delete the data we have collected or stored about you, email colt3211@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your Meta display name or username in the Game so we can find your records. We will confirm your request and delete the personal data we hold about you, including any leaderboard entries and backend player records, within 30 days, then tell you when it is done. We may keep limited information only where the law requires it or to prevent fraud and abuse. Real-time multiplayer data is not stored after a session ends, so there is nothing to delete for it. Data held by Meta or other third-party services is governed by their own policies, and you can manage or delete it through your Meta account settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than your own. By playing the Game, you understand that your information may be transferred to and processed in those countries.

10. Changes to this policy
